Biodiversity

With over 330 lakes and thousands of hectares of forests and wetlands, Greater Sudbury is home to a rich diversity of plants, animals and ecosystems. Our world-renowned regreening efforts have achieved significant improvements on land affected by mining and smelting operations; however, there is still more work to be done.
