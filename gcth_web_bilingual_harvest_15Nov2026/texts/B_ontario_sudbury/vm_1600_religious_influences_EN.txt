Religious Influences

The spiritual face of Sudbury is as diverse as the cultures that make up the community. From Sudbury's early days as a mining town to the present day, religion has served as an integral part of the lives of residents.

Mainstream religions such as Anglicanism, Protestantism, and Catholicism, have been present in the community since the town's inception, but along with these, many other faiths have found a home in Sudbury.
