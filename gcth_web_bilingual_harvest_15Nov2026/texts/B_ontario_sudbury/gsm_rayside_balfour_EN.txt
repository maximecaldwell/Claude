Rayside-Balfour Museum

The Rayside-Balfour Museum museum is located in Azilda’s Gilles Pelland Library and displays artifacts relating to the development and history of the communities which make up the former Town of Rayside-Balfour. It also highlights some of the people who played a key role in the community.

The temporary exhibition, Reflections of Whitewater Lake, is on display until summer of 2025. See beautiful photos of the lake, learn about the lake’s fish and aviation history, see vintage lures, locally carved duck decoys and learn about ice lake safety!

Admission is free.
