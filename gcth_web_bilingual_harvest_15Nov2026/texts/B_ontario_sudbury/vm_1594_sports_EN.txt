Sports

Sudbury has a rich history of involvement in many different types of sports since the establishment of the town over 120 years ago. Sports have served as an escape from everyday hardships and have provided residents with engaging and entertaining social activities in which to partake.

Although mostly dominated by men, women have also made incredible strides in fostering the development of sporting events in the community.

Curling, lacrosse, baseball, hockey, football, and soccer are but a few of the many popular sports still enjoyed by Sudburians today. The history of these sports and the people who played them serve to instill pride in our community's ability to compete against the best in the world and win.
