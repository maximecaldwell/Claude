Levack

The Town of Levack has an interesting and unique history which no other community in the City of Greater Sudbury can compare to. From the creation of the jitney, to the gravel trains that operated in the 1940's, Levack residents have always been innovative people capable of generating a solution to any problem.

While the Town of Levack may be small in size, the people of this close-knit community have always been big at heart. Their willingness to help a neighbour and their dedication to the growth and prosperity of their town serves as a shining example of how we all should live.
