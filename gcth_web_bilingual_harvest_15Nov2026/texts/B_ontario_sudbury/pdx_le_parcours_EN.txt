Discover Place des Arts

Self-Guided Tours

Wander about and admire Place des Arts! Take a stroll through the building, its halls, wings and corridors, and appreciate the artistic and architectural embellishments you’ll encounter.

Guided Tours

Go on a journey through the Franco-Ontarian culture and heritage of Nouvel-Ontario. This brand new building is a direct descendant of the cultural effervescence that has been taking place for the past fifty years. The architectural design of the building, as well as the materials and objects it features, are intended to bear witness to this rich heritage and to pay tribute to it.
