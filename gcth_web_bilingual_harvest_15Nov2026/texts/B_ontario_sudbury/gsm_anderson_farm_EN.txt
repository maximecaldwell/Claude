Museum staff are responsible for four physical museum locations, so they may not always be on site at the Anderson Farm Museum.

To learn more about events and programs hosted at Anderson Farm, visit our Upcoming Events and Programs page.

Parking at the Anderson Farm Museum is free.

Parking is available in front of the shipping container and the Little Free Library. Accessible parking is available on the left-hand side of the regular parking lot and to the left of the Milkhouse building.

During some large events, parking is not permitted on site. Please always check event information for special directions pertaining to parking or contact Museum staff.
