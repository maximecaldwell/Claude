Falconbridge

The community of Falconbridge is deeply connected to the Falconbridge mining company. The history of both company and town are so completely intertwined that one could not exist without the other.

This unique connection has served both company and town for many years. Falconbridge Nickel Mines has provided the community with a school, a place to worship, businesses, and sport facilities, while the people of Falconbridge have given the corporation loyalty and dedication for numerous years.

From its roots as a true company town to its status as part of the City of Greater Sudbury, the Town of Falconbridge has developed into a warm and welcoming community that enjoys a tradition of good neighbours and friendly faces.
