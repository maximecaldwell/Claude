Carrefour francophone de Sudbury

Carrefour francophone is Greater Sudbury’s cultural and community centre. They oversee 11 Early learning centres, 7 Tremplins (before and after school programs), a series of cultural summer camps, the Clé de Sol music school, as well as shows presented under La Slague!
