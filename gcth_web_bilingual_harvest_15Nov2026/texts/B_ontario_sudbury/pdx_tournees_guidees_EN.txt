Discover Place des Arts

These guided tours of Place des Arts take you on a journey through the Franco-Ontarian culture and heritage of Nouvel-Ontario. This brand new building is a direct descendant of the cultural effervescence that has been taking place for the past fifty years. The architectural design of the building, as well as the materials and objects it features, are intended to bear witness to this rich heritage and to pay tribute to it.
