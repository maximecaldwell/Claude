Celebrating Greater Sudbury's Regreening Efforts with Dr. Jane Goodall

Jul 7, 2022

Dr. Jane Goodall, Founder of the Jane Goodall Institute and UN Messenger of Peace, was joined by the Right Honourable Justin Trudeau, Prime Minister of Canada, to plant the 10 millionth tree on the beautiful shores of Ramsey Lake in celebration of Greater Sudbury's regreening efforts. Over the past 43 years, the City of Greater Sudbury has made a miraculous transformation from a moonscape to a living landscape. Its world-renowned, award-winning Regreening program has enabled partners and thousands of residents to play a role in the ecological recovery of the community. Since 1978, the City has engaged in regreening its landscape by planting ten million tree seedlings through the City's Regreening program with oversight by VETAC, City Council’s Regreening Advisory Panel. As a result, Greater Sudbury is a leading global example of how scientists, government, industry, and the broader community can work together to restore landscapes. Greater Sudbury’s landscape transformation through local regreening continues to significantly improve the quality of life for the community and future generations. The City of Greater Sudbury is proud to host Dr. Jane Goodall in partnership with Science North for the upcoming Jane Goodall IMAX film "Reasons for Hope" to celebrate and tell the story of Greater Sudbury’s regreening achievements.

Quotes: "I first visited Sudbury to learn about the fantastic greening project over 20 years ago. I planted a few trees and released a fish in a once polluted stream. The success of this magnificent effort was made possible because of the citizens of Sudbury and their commitment to conservation and the creation of a healthy environment. The regreening of Sudbury is a shining example of why I have hope – the resilience of nature and the indomitable spirit that tackles what seems impossible and will not give up." – Dr. Jane Goodall, Founder of the Jane Goodall Institute and UN Messenger of Peace

“What Sudbury has achieved through its decades-long Regreening Program is an example for Canada and the world. Our government is supporting this initiative alongside our own commitment to plant two billion trees across Canada in the coming decade. Together, we can protect and restore nature, and build a healthy future for generations to come.” – The Right Honourable Justin Trudeau, Prime Minister of Canada
