THE BIG IMPACT SHOW

* Included in the Next-Generation Mine Tour *

Get ready for a story that’s a billions of years in the making with the The Big Impact Show. At the end of your tour, you’ll be immersed in a multimedia journey that brings Sudbury’s remarkable geological story to life.

Projected directly on to a wall of rocks underground, you'll witness the massive meteorite impact that shaped the Sudbury Basin and created one of the richest ore deposits in the world. Through sound, light, movement, and special effects, discover how this extraordinary geological event influenced the region’s landscapes, minerals, mining history, and the everyday materials we rely on today.

Seizure warning: Tour contains flashing lights. Closed captioning is available and displayed in the language of the presentation.
