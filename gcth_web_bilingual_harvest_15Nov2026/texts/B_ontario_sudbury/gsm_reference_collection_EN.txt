The Greater Sudbury Museums Reference Collection

The Greater Sudbury Museums Reference Collection is a collection of print material, primarily focused on local and Canadian history, and which includes some historical publications.

How to Access These Resources

These publications are not available to borrow, and they may only be accessed in person at the Anderson Farm Museum.

Greater Sudbury Public Library

Greater Sudbury Public Library branches hold copies of the majority of publications in this collection. The library’s collections can be searched through their online catalogue.

– When library catalogue search results are displayed, click the title of the desired publication for more information.

– Then click “Availability by Location” (below the call number) to view Greater Sudbury Public Library branches that also hold publication.
