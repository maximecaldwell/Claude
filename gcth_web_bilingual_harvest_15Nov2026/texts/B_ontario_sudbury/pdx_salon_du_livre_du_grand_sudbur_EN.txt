Salon du livre du Grand Sudbury

Founded in 2004 and incorporated in 2005, the Salon du livre du Grand Sudbury (SLGS) hosts a French-language literary season for Francophones and Francophiles in Northeastern Ontario, targeting a variety of audiences.
