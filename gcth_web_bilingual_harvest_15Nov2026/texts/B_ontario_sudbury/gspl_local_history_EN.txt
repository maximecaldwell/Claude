Local History

Step into the past and explore the history of Greater Sudbury through the Library’s Local History resources and the Mary C. Shantz Local History Collection at the Main Library. Named for Mary C. Shantz, head of the Reference Department at the Sudbury Public Library from 1966 to 1984, the collection exists thanks to her early efforts to gather and preserve local photographs, reports, books, and community publications, laying the foundation for what has become a significant resource for the region.

Today, the collection brings together a wide range of materials documenting the people, places, and events that have shaped Greater Sudbury. From photographs and maps to newspapers, reports, and community publications, these resources offer many ways to explore and better understand the story of the community through both in‑library collections and online resources.

Visit the Collection

Explore materials available in‑library through the Mary C. Shantz Local History Collection, located on the Reference Level of the Main Library (74 Mackenzie Street). These resources include print publications, research and reference materials, and unique items that document the people, places, and development of Greater Sudbury.

Reference staff are available to assist with locating materials, using resources, and accessing microfilm. While many items are available for in‑library use, some local history books can be borrowed through the Library’s regular circulating collection.

Visit the Main Library to explore or Contact us for more information.
