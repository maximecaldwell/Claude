Ugliest Schoolyard Contest

Greater Sudbury’s Ugliest Schoolyard Contest was hosted by VETAC, City Council’s Advisory Panel on Regreening, from 2005 to 2022 to help local schools create greener and healthier schoolyards. During that time, a total of 49 different schools received funding to regreen their schoolyards.

See Greater Sudbury’s ugliest schoolyard makeovers on our interactive map or watch the video overview.
