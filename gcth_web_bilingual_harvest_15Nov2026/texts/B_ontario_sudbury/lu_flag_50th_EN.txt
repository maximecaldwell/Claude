50th anniversary of the creation of the Franco-Ontarian flag

September 25, 2025 • 8:15 AM - 12 PM

Founder's Square

The AEF invites you to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the creation of the Franco-Ontarian flag.

On September 25, the day will begin with an official ceremony and end with a free concert at Founders’ Square. Throughout the day, several giveaways will be available, with a total value of about $1,000. For more information, keep an eye on our Instagram: @aef.ul.
