Health

We aim to operate safe workplaces and to enhance the health and well-being of employees, contractors and communities. We maintain a safe workplace based on mutual respect, fairness and integrity, and are guided by our Health & Safety Policy.

We focus on the health of our people so we can support their long-term well-being, and because we understand that a fit, healthy workforce is a safer workforce that better propels our journey to zero harm.

One such example of our commitment to the health and well-being of our people is the HealthyWork program currently in place at Nickel Rim South (NRS).

Designed and implemented by Med-I-Well Services, a nationally recognized organization that provides industrial health care solutions, HealthyWork comprises a variety of programs and services for NRS employees and their families covering strength training, nutrition, smoking cessation, healthy retirement, stress management and more.

"I just want to say how appreciative I am of this whole program [HealthyWork]. If this had not been offered through work, I wouldn’t have done it. Who has time to go see a nutrition specialist or an exercise consultant outside of work? There is just such a focus on health and wellness here that is unique to this company."
