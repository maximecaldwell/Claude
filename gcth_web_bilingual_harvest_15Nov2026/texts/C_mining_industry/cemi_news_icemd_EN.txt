CEMI and ICEMD Forge Partnership to Advance Indigenous Leadership in Mining Innovation

Sudbury, Ontario – September 16, 2025 – The Centre for Excellence in Mining Innovation (CEMI), which manages the Mining Innovation Commercialization Accelerator (MICA) Network, announced a new partnership with the Indigenous Centre for Excellence in Mineral Development (ICEMD). The agreement, signed in Sudbury, Ontario, on September 16, 2025, is designed to strengthen Indigenous leadership and participation within Canada's mining innovation ecosystem.

Through this agreement, CEMI and ICEMD will work together to align priorities, co-develop innovation projects that enhance community well-being, and ensure Indigenous perspectives are central to the advancement of mining technologies. The partnership will see the two organizations co-host webinars, provide community briefings on emerging mining technologies, and create youth outreach programs to inspire the next generation of leaders in mining innovation.

CEMI and ICEMD will also co-develop white papers, ESG dashboards, and digital community platforms to support transparency, trust, and knowledge-sharing. In addition, both organizations will collaborate to elevate Indigenous voices and highlight Northern Ontario’s role in shaping global mining innovation. Importantly, the partnership is grounded in a shared commitment to uphold OCAP® principles (Ownership, Control, Access, and Possession) in the stewardship of First Nations data.

“This partnership reflects CEMI’s commitment to ensuring that mining innovation contributes to inclusive and sustainable development across Canada.
