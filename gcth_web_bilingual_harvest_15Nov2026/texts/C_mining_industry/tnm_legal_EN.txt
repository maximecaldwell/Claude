General Rules

Contest Overview

The contest (the “Contest”) is organized by The Northern Miner, a subsidiary of EarthLabs Inc. (TSX-V: SPOT).

The Total Prize Pool is 289 one-ounce gold coins valued at approximately CAD$1,335,637, comprised as follows:

- Grand Prize: 217 one-ounce gold coins (~CAD$1,000,000)

- 12 Monthly Bonus Prizes: 72 one-ounce gold coins total (6 coins or ~CAD$27,729 per prize)

The Grand Prize and each Bonus Prize will be located in one or more provinces or territories of Canada.

Gold Valuation Details:

All valuations are based on the spot market gold price as of May 28, 2025 (USD$3,342.65 per coin) and a CAD/USD exchange rate of 1.3826, resulting in a per-coin value of CAD$4,621.55. Coins are one-ounce gold and are not intended for circulation under the Royal Canadian Mint Act.

Treasure Format:

Each prize is tied to a physical Treasure Box hidden somewhere in Canada containing inside a unique alphanumeric code.

Redemption:

Participants must locate the hidden Treasure Box, as provided for in the Official Rules and Regulations, in order to redeem the Grand Prize or a Bonus Prize.

Redemption requires:

- Submission of the unique alphanumeric code via the submission link.

- Upload of a photo showing the claimed Treasure Box or location

- Provision of full name, valid email address, and telephone number for verification purposes
Acceptance of the Official Rules and Regulations.

The first eligible Participant to correctly submit a valid code and meet all of the requirements in the Official Rules and Regulations as determined by the Organizer in its sole and absolute discretion, will be eligible to claim the relevant Prize.

The Contest is open to individuals who reside in Canada and who are (i) 18 years of age or older, or (ii) of legal age in the province or territory in which the individual resides.

All winners are required to complete a winner’s declaration and release and may be subject to identity verification.

Contest Period:

The Contest will start on August 13, 2025 and will continue until 12:00 midnight on December 31, 2026. All claims must be made by 12:00 midnight, December 31, 2026. If a claim is not made by December 31, 2026, the claim will be forfeited and the prize will not be awarded.

5) How to Play

Clue Access & Distribution

Grand Prize

A multi-stanza poem will be published on https://treasure.northernminer.com. This poem contains the core clues to locate the Treasure Box containing a unique alphanumeric code that is required to claim the Grand Prize of $1,000,000 in gold coins.

A Participant must accept and agree to the Official Rule and Regulations of the Contest before viewing the poem. No purchase is necessary to participate.

Bonus Prizes

Each Bonus Prize will be associated with its own unique poem published on https://treasure.northernminer.com. Bonus Prize poems and clues will be released each month.

Bonus Clues

Bonus clues for both the Grand and the Bonus Prizes may be distributed through EarthLabs media channels:

- The Northern Miner (print & online)

- MINING.COM

- Canadian Mining Journal

- CEO.CA

- CrashLabs Podcast

- EarthLabs Expeditions

Subscribers to The Northern Miner and EarthLabs other media channels will have access to bonus clues on the day of their release and to a consolidation of the bonus clues. Non-subscribers may request a bonus clue by emailing treasure@northernminer.com on or after the day of the release of the bonus clue.

All Treasure Boxes are hidden within Canada. They will be physically accessible on foot and are located on public property. Participants will not require any special gear, digging tools, metal detectors, or similar equipment. Participants should dress appropriately for the environment and conditions. Participants must conduct their search in a safe, respectful, and non-commercial manner.

Participants are strictly prohibited from using heavy equipment, digging tools, or similar devices; metal detectors, where prohibited by law or regulation; or operating drones in restricted airspace or protected areas. Commercial searching operations are not permitted under any circumstances. This commercial search operations prohibition includes, but is not limited to, organized expeditions, paid tours, or any activity for commercial gain. Participants may not sell or distribute clues, maps, or other search-related materials.

Participants must not search while under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or any substance that could impair judgment or reaction time. Reckless or dangerous searching behaviour is not permitted. This prohibition includes, but is not limited to, entering hazardous terrain, searching in extreme weather, failing to wear appropriate clothing or footwear, or engaging in any conduct that could damage property or the environment.

Participants are not permitted to trespass. Participation in the Contest does not excuse unlawful entry or provide permission to access land without proper authorization. If a Participant enters property unlawfully or causes damage to the property or the environment, the Participant is fully responsible for such damage and agrees to indemnify the Organizer and all Released Parties for any resulting harm or liability.

Any conduct that endangers people, disrespects property, or undermines the integrity of the Contest may result in immediate disqualification at the sole discretion of the Organizer.

Participants enter property at their own risk and search the property for the applicable Treasure Box at their own risk. The Organizers and Released Parties are not liable for any accident, death, injury, damage, loss or other harm incurred as a result of the Participant entering the property and searching the property for the Treasure Box. Participants must comply with applicable laws and with any requirements of the property owner or occupant of the property.

Participants are solely responsible for any costs or expenses incurred to participate in, arising from or associated with participation, such costs and expenses include but are not limited to the costs of travel to and from any location, the costs of any accommodation, meals or transportation, the costs of any clothing, equipment or other items that the Participant purchases, and the costs of any damaged or lost equipment, clothing or other items.
