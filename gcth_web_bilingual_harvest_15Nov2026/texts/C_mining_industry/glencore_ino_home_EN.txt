Sudbury INO

Sudbury, Ontario, Canada is one of the most geologically rich areas of the world. This northern city in the Canadian Shield boasts a 60-kilometre-long formation called the Sudbury basin, which is brimming with nickel, copper, cobalt, gold, silver, platinum and palladium deposits.

It’s also been home to Sudbury Integrated Nickel Operations since 1929.

Our Sudbury operations include exploration, Fraser Mine, Craig Mine Onaping Depth (Project), Nickel Rim South Mine (Care and Maintenance), Strathcona Mill, and the Smelter, all located in this basin. We currently employ more than 1,200 permanent employees and support another 1,200 contractors.
