Two Numbers Worksheet

Every number the clues carry, in one place, for Max, Mike and Dad. Chapters are in release order. Gold marks the digits each chapter carried that its own bonus box didn’t need. Those are the most likely pieces of the "two numbers".

The instruction

SEEK THE DIGITS … FROM EVERY CHAPTER, EVERY POST … TWO NUMBERS MARK THE TREASURE’S BASE

Sean Boyd’s card, grand-prize clue 5 (video, 15 Oct 2026). From launch week, the site called the poem plus the monthly clues "the Hunt Marker – the keys to the gold". The only bonus clue excluded from the grand prize is the 4-line Golden Triangle riddle.

7. Already tried

Each came out no better than random digits. Listed so nobody repeats them without a new reason.

  • Map coordinates from the chapter codes in release order and in clock order, split at every point (decimal and degrees-minutes-seconds).
  • Article features as digits: subheads, title punctuation, last digit of word count, paragraph counts, English and French (11 versions).
  • 6 + 6 splits of the chapters: first half vs second, odd vs even, east vs west, by poem stanza, English vs French; first, last, total or whole digits; four map readings (760 tries, 17 Nov).
  • The clocks as an ordering or compass bearings; the Alt-code weave; a survey-marker ID; poem letter and word counts as coordinates.
  • Sudbury leaf as letters or as word positions in every clue text, English and French.
  • Not yet tried: keeping only some digits from each chapter by a rule someone chooses (your record’s "Rule A"); lot and concession numbers on a township map; the 20 · 60 · 5 recap openers.
