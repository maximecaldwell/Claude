FAQ Update

We’re also posting the following update to our FAQ on our website:

The Dawson Prize
Neither The Northern Miner nor EarthLabs or the Sponsors of the Great Canadian Treasure Hunt have knowledge of the specific hiding places of any prizes. The individual who claimed the Dawson Prize did not disclose the precise location, which we respect as part of the mystery of the hunt.

How was it verified?
All prizes are verified through the official claim process. In this case, the winner submitted both a photo and the correct claim code through the claim form, which our team confirmed shortly before announcing the result. Our goal is to issue a press release and announce winners within 24–48 hours of successful submission and verification.

Other common questions

Have any of the other treasures been found?
No. To date, only Dawson has been successfully claimed and verified. We have not received any other verified claims through the Treasure Claim form.

Does the Grand Prize poem contain information about the Bonus Prizes?
The Grand Prize treasure poem and the Bonus Prize clues are separate. Each Bonus Prize has its own dedicated set of clues. Any further interpretation is part of the challenge and the fun of the hunt.

Is there a set schedule for releasing Bonus Prize clues?
Clues are released monthly, although exact dates may vary. Subscribers will continue to receive these clues in advance of the general public.
