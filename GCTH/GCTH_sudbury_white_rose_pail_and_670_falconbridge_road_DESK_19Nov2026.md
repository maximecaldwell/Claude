## 1. The pail (already in the record)
- **Where it appears:** the Sudbury clue video, at about 33.5–35.8 s EN, in the left half of the split screen. An underground driller works at a column drill, with a pail at his feet.
- **Its lettering:** read on 18 Nov as `..TE RO..` (`GCTH_sudbury_video_all_visible_numbers_sweep_DESK_18Nov2026.md`, row 6). The 18 Nov identification file calls it a **White Rose pail**.
- **The photo's source:** *Inco Triangle*, Sep 1948, p. 16, "Diamond Drilling Underground at Garson Mine". It matches at 196 points, the pail included (`GCTH_sudbury_video_unplaced_photos_identification_DESK_18Nov2026.md`).
- **So the pail is part of the 1948 photo,** not something added by the clue-makers. White Rose was Canadian Oil Companies' oil and gas brand until 1962.

## 2. The White Rose store (new)
- **The chain:** White Rose Home and Garden Centres / White Rose Crafts. It was an Ontario chain that went bankrupt in 2002; one secondary source says it ended about 2005.
- **The Sudbury store, from White Rose's own archived store list** (`whiterose.ca/stores/northern.html`, Wayback captures 2000, 2001 and 2003; saved in `~/GCTH/raw/white_rose_sudbury_19Nov2026/`):
  > SUDBURY — 670 Falconbridge Road, Sudbury, Ontario P3A 4S4 · T: 705-524-7555 · "Lasalle Blvd to Falconbridge Road (Hwy) … store is located on south east corner of Falconbridge Road and Chalmers".
- **It closed** in one of the chain's waves of Ontario closures; the Globe and Mail lists Sudbury among nine stores shut.
- **Today:** 670 Falconbridge Road (Unit 2) is **Surplus Furniture and Mattress Warehouse**, in the former Leisure Days RV building (Yellow Pages, BBB, surplusfurniture.com).
- **Position:** Chalmers Street is at about 46.5139 N, 80.9272 W (Nominatim), so the store's corner is about 46.513 N, 80.927 W. That is New Sudbury, McKim Township (city township code 32), lot 11, concession 5.
