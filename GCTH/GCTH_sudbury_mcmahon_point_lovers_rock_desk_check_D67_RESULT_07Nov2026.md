## Why it is on the list at all (carried, with its weaknesses)
- **For it:** Whitewater Lake is the namesake of the **Whitewater Group**, the crater-fill rocks: a Kleanza-style name pun for a geology audience (6 Sep addendum). The 3 Nov recap adds two geology lines not in the original article ("Could that cosmic collision lead you to gold here on Earth?" / "Can Sudbury's extraordinary geological history point you toward hidden gold?"). **That reading is post hoc** (D66 PREREG §1 recorded S3 and did not select with it). There are zero hunter reports since release.
- **Against it:** no interpretive signage; 1.13× the 41 km map envelope; restoration filter probably fails (no smelter or regreened land known to be in view); no clue text names Azilda, Whitewater or the Valley.

## Field card (if Max goes)
- **Park at:** Whitewater Lake Park, 535 Laurier St W, Azilda. Walk south along the shore past the beach (46.5429, −81.1328) and the boat launch (46.5418, −81.1321) to **McMahon Point (46.5395, −81.1324)**.
- **Stay inside the park strip.** Cottages and the trailer park are nearby; anything outside Centennial Park is private.
- **Search pattern:** the knoll top and its lake-facing side first. Then rock clefts, the bases of the largest trees, and any spot with a view back across the lake. "Above the earth yet tucked away": not buried.
- **Photograph** any sign, marker or plaque face in full (both languages if bilingual).
- **November:** short daylight; slippery rock at the waterline; stay off any ice.
