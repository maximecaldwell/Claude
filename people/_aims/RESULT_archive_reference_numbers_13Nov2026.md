# Archives of Ontario: reference codes vs the Sudbury leaf numbers, and Gowganda photos · RESULT · 13 Nov 2026

**Follows:** R-1961 (archive reference numbers starting 6-2 / 3-2, never actually searched) and R-1979 (the Gowganda King Edward Hotel photo).
**Evidence label:** `desk_data`. Source: the Archives of Ontario's own catalogue exports on Ontario's open-data portal (data.ontario.ca), as of May 2020:
- *Archives Descriptive Guide* (22,831 fonds, series and sub-series descriptions)
- *Archives of Ontario photographs and maps* (24,584 item-level images)

Together that is 44,687 distinct reference codes. Holdings described after May 2020 are not in these exports.

## 1. Reference codes against the leaf numbers: negative

- **Leaf numbers tested:** English 6 | 2 | 76 | 37 | 3 | 25 | 69 | 4 | 20; French 3 | 2 | 58 | 16 | 7 | 14 | 24 | 25 | 3.
- **Best match:** no code goes past the first two numbers. There is no `x 6-2-76…` and no `x 3-2-58…`. The only matches are two groups deep:

| Code | What it is |
|---|---|
| RG 6-2 | Correspondence of the Treasurer of Ontario, 1905–1971 |
| F 6-2 | William H. Hearst correspondence |
| C 6-2 | Photographs of the play "Distrust", 1891–92 |
| RG 3-2 | Premier James Whitney office records, 1897–1914 |
| F 3-2 | Oliver Mowat correspondence |
| C 3-2 | Gilbert A. Milne prints of Mackenzie King on Parliament Hill |

- **Chance baseline:**
  - 79 of the 81 possible pairs from 1-1 to 9-9 exist as the start of some code, so a two-number match is expected for almost any pair.
  - Random 9-number sequences drawn from the same digits reach a two-deep match 70 % of the time and a three-deep match 3 % of the time.
  - The real leaf numbers do no better than chance.
- **Verdict:** closed for the Archives of Ontario catalogue as of 2020. Library and Archives Canada was not tested: its search refuses automated requests (its page says the restriction is because of bot load), so it was not scraped.

- **No King Edward Hotel:** no Gowganda King Edward Hotel appears anywhere in the 24,584 catalogued images. Every "King Edward Hotel" item is the Toronto hotel (1924–1950).
- **F 4594** (Gowganda mining photographic collection, 59 photos, mostly 1909) is the fonds already enumerated in R-0676.
- **Other Gowganda items:** two Crown-land plans of the Gowganda Lake west-side subdivision, Nicol Township (RG 1-100-0-0-1982 and RG 1-100-0-0-2725), and the Tim Bakker photographs, 1948–53 (F 1405-12-115).
