## 4. Archives: the reference-number question (R-1961) and the Gowganda photo (R-1979)

**Source:** the Archives of Ontario's own catalogue exports on data.ontario.ca, as of May 2020: the *Archives Descriptive Guide* (22,831 descriptions) and *Photographs and maps* (24,584 images). That is 44,687 reference codes. Full write-up: `~/GCTH/people/RESULT_archive_reference_numbers_13Nov2026.md`.

**Leaf numbers as reference-code prefixes: negative**
- No code goes past two groups. There is no `x 6-2-76…` and no `x 3-2-58…`.
- The only two-group matches are unrelated: RG 6-2 (Treasurer of Ontario correspondence), F 6-2 (Hearst), C 6-2 (photos of an 1891 play), RG 3-2 (Premier Whitney's office), F 3-2 (Mowat) and C 3-2 (Milne prints of Mackenzie King).
- **Chance baseline:**
  - 79 of the 81 pairs 1-1 to 9-9 exist as the start of some code.
  - Random 9-number sequences built from the same digits reach a two-deep match 70 % of the time and a three-deep match 3 % of the time.
  - The real leaf numbers do no better than chance.
- **Verdict:** closed for the Archives of Ontario catalogue (as of 2020).

**Gowganda: a series the record had never seen**
- **RG 13-30-1-10** (Department of Mines and Northern Affairs, ca. 1909) holds three images:
  - "Dog team in Gowganda" (I0004665)
  - "Gowganda street scene" (I0004666)
  - "Post office and bank in Gowganda" (I0004667)
- They are probably the same government photos as the 1909 Bureau of Mines report plates already ruled out (R-2056). That is unconfirmed until someone looks at the images.
- **No Gowganda King Edward Hotel is anywhere in the 24,584 Archives of Ontario images.** Every "King Edward Hotel" item is the Toronto hotel (1924–1950).
- Also present: Crown-land plans of the Gowganda Lake west-side subdivision, Nicol Township (RG 1-100-0-0-1982 and RG 1-100-0-0-2725), and the Tim Bakker photographs, 1948–53 (F 1405-12-115).

**Library and Archives Canada (LAC)**
- **Not searched automatically.** Its search refuses automated browsers (a "nobots" 403), even after the human check, and its notice says the restriction is because of bot load. That was respected and not worked around.
- The remaining LAC work has to happen in Max's own Chrome, or through LAC's **Collection data export tool**, which is its sanctioned bulk route.

**Correction to this chat's own earlier message:** it called the Archives of Ontario F 4594 enquiry "still open". It was not: F 4594 was enumerated 59/59 on 19 Sep with no hotel photo (R-0676). The BX0001/FD/FL filenames are TNM's own scans (R-0248).
